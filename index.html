// A FIRST Robotics community initiative

Robotics teams, put to work for your school.

EduEngineering turns robotics teams into an on-campus build shop — fixing, building, and creating whatever teachers and staff need, for free. Started by FRC Team Σ-Motion, now open to any team that wants to run it.

01 — THE MODEL

Three moving parts.

The same system Team Σ-Motion has run at Rancho Bernardo High School for years — now something any team can spin up for their own campus in an afternoon.

01

A request form for your school

Teachers and staff submit what they need — no login required. Post the QR code around campus and requests land straight in your dashboard.

02

Your own private dashboard

Your team registers, gets approved, and gets a dashboard that shows only your school's requests — nobody else's, and nobody sees yours.

03

Build it, mark it done

Distribute requests across your subteam by experience level, track status as you go, and close the loop with the teacher who asked.

02 — JOIN THE NETWORK

Bring EduEngineering to your team.

Registration takes two minutes. A network admin reviews and approves new teams before your dashboard goes live — this keeps request data private to real, verified teams.
